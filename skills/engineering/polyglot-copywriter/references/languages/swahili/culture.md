## Natural grammar

1. **SVO default** — keep natural.
2. **ni copula** — `ni undefined`.
3. **ha- negation** on verbs.
4. **kwenye for location** — `kwenye .env`.
5. **Plenary subject prefixes** — a-, wa-, i- agreement.
6. **Shauri for suggestions** — `tuendeshe test`.
7. **Avoid English prepositions** calqued.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: Jumanne 16 Sep 2026
