# hausa registers

Three registers. Default is `santai`. Casual is **not** automatically slang.

| Register | Config key | When |
|---|---|---|
| Formal / careful | `baku` | Official letters, legal, investor — on request |
| Work | `profesional` | Email, docs, LinkedIn — on request |
| Casual | `santai` | **Default**: chat, explanations, everyday writing |

## Pronouns and address

ni / mu; kai (m) / ke (f); su.

## Examples

### `baku` (formal)

- **Good:** Muna sanar da cewa sabis zai tsaya ranar Talata don gyara.
- **Bad:** Service will be down — English pasted.

### `profesional` (work)

- **Good:** `API_TOKEN` ba ya cikin `.env`, don haka `loadConfig` yana dawo da `undefined`.
- **Bad:** Kindly revert.

### `santai` (casual)

- **Good:** `API_TOKEN` ba ya cikin `.env` — gudanar `npm test -- config`.
- **Bad:** Fake accent spelling.

## What casual is not

- Not English every noun
- Not religious insults
