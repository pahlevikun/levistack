## Natural grammar

1. **SVO basic** — subject focus early.
2. **ba negation** — `ba ya aiki`.
3. **a cikin for in** — `a cikin .env`.
4. **Gender agreement** on kai/ke.
5. **Imperative plural polite** — `mu yi` for let's.
6. **Aspect markers** — `yana`, `ya` past.
7. **Don't drop tone meaning** in minimal pairs when typing.
