# elvish registers

Three registers. Default is `santai`. Casual is **not** automatically slang.

| Register | Config key | When |
|---|---|---|
| Formal / careful | `baku` | Official letters, legal, investor — on request |
| Work | `profesional` | Email, docs, LinkedIn — on request |
| Casual | `santai` | **Default**: chat, explanations, everyday writing |

## Pronouns and address

Use Indonesian/English fallback pronouns.

## Examples

### `baku` (formal)

- **Good:** Jelaskan batas: Elvish tidak didukung untuk draf; lanjut dalam Indonesia netral.
- **Bad:** Invented Quenya or Sindarin words.

### `profesional` (work)

- **Good:** Same — honest fallback with clear technical content.
- **Bad:** Tolkien roleplay or fake Elvish archaisms.

### `santai` (casual)

- **Good:** Bahasa Indonesia netral setelah satu kalimat batas.
- **Bad:** Fake Elvish greetings or rune flourishes.

## What casual is not

- Not inventing
- Not extended fantasy or fandom roleplay
