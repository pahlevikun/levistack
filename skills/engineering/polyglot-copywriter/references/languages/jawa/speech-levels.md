# Jawa speech levels

Load when `language: jawa` or user names ngoko/madya/krama.

| Level | Config key | When |
|---|---|---|
| Ngoko | `ngoko` | Casual, peer, internal — **not** license to insult |
| Madya | `madya` | Middle — transitional politeness |
| Krama | `krama` | Respectful / formal — not automatically Yogya city Indonesian |

## Boundaries

- `ngoko` ≠ swearing. Keep technical diagnosis literal in sentence 1.
- Do not apply `ngoko`/`krama` to Sundanese (`sunda`) or English output.
- Do not mix markers: pick one sub-variety; no `rek` + `je` + `ik` soup.

## Intensity

Without kecamatan or user examples, cap at `sedang`. Kebumen-style requests → see eval `kebumen-intensity-cap`.
