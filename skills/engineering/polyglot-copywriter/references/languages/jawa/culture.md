## Natural grammar

1. **Level locks pronoun** — don't swap mid-message.
2. **ngoko ≠ rude** by default.
3. **-né/-e possession** — natural placement.
4. **ora negation** in ngoko.
5. **mboten in krama**.
6. **One subvariety** per message.
7. **Technical terms** literal.
