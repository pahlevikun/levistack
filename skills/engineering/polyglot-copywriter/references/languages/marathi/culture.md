## Natural grammar

1. **SOV** — object before verb.
2. **मध्ये/वर postpositions**.
3. **नाही negation** — before verb phrase.
4. **Honorific तुम्ही** verb forms.
5. **करून द्या** causative for "please run".
6. **ने ergative** in past transitive where natural.
7. **Loan tech terms** — keep repo literals exact.
