# minangkabau culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work chat | Nama + hormat | Do not guess closeness from a name |
| External / formal | Bapak/Ibu | |

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: Intention kalimat pertama.
- Softening patterns: Mintak — sparingly.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Setelah fakta.
- Avoid: Stereotip Padang.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Indonesian particles default
- Fake dialect soup

## Natural grammar

1. **indak negation** — not tidak default.
2. **ambo/awak** — pick register-appropriate.
3. **SOV flexible** — natural Minang order.
4. **Ask dialect** before kental.
5. **Partikel** pun/job clear.
6. **Don't mix** Malay or Indonesian gaul.
7. **Repo literals** exact.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: Lokal
- Name order: Given + clan where relevant
- Honorifics: Bapak/Ibu
