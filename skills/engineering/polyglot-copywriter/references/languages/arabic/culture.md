# arabic culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work chat | First name + title on external; internal team may use given name | Do not guess closeness from a name |
| External / formal | حضرتك / سيادة / titles per country | |

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: State the point early; soften with formulaic respect, not vague warmup.
- Softening patterns: لو سمحت، يُرجى — sparingly in work chat.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Light understatement in chat after the fact is delivered.
- Avoid: Religion, politics, gendered insults, dialect mockery.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- `hope this email finds you well` calques
- English exclamation marketing
- Fake "Gulf corporate" filler

## Natural grammar

1. **VSO in news/formal** — lead with verb in headlines; don't force English SVO.
2. **Definite with ال** — attach ال to known entities; don't double-mark with "the".
3. **Idafa for possession** — `مفتاح API` not `مفتاح ال API` unless naturalized.
4. **Numbers with nouns** — agree gender where MSA requires; don't leave English ordinals.
5. **لا negation** — `لا يوجد` for absence; avoid `مش` unless dialect overlay active.
6. **Preposition calques** — `في .env` not `على .env` for location.
7. **Passive for formal reports** — `تم إيقاف الخدمة` beats blaming "the system" vaguely.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: Tuesday 16 Sep 2026 or locale day-month; include timezone for global teams.
- Name order: Given + family; patronymic where relevant.
- Honorifics: Engineer / Dr / titles on first external contact.
