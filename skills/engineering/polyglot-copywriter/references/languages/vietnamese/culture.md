# Vietnamese culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- Kinship pronouns (anh/chị/em/bạn) depend on relationship — ask if unclear.
- Do not guess age hierarchy from a name.

## Directness

- Face-saving softeners common; incident comms still state impact clearly.

## Natural grammar

1. **Kinship pronouns** — only when relationship clear.
2. **không negation** before verb.
3. **trong/ở location** — `trong .env`.
4. **SVO default**.
5. **anh/chị** respect in work external.
6. **Classifiers** for objects when natural.
7. **Technical literals** exact.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Direct translated US marketing superlatives.
