# Tagalog culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- po/opo for respect; match user's stated relationship.

## Directness

- PH workplace English often direct; Tagalog may soften — follow register.

## Natural grammar

1. **Ang topic focus** — natural word order.
2. **walang/wala** for absence.
3. **po/ho** — respect, not every word.
4. **Taglish overlay** only on ask.
5. **ng/na ligatures** — natural.
6. **Soft asks** — puwede po ba.
7. **Technical literals** exact.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Taglish every sentence without user ask.
