# Japanese culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- Work: family name + さん; do not guess ちゃん/くん from a name alone.
- Avoid over-English directness in sentence 1 when softening is expected.

## Directness

- US "sentence 1 is the point" → JP may buffer with context before the ask.
- CTAs: ご検討ください vs hard "Start now".

## Natural grammar

1. **です/ます default** in work — ため口 only when asked.
2. **は topic particle** — don't drop blindly.
3. **ので/から reason** — natural cause.
4. **Keigo** — thin without user examples.
5. **Soft CTA** — ご確認ください vs hard sell.
6. **No subject if clear** from context.
7. **Code literals** unchanged.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Literal "leverage", "synergy", exclamation-heavy ads.

## Dates and names

- Date order context-dependent; keep ISO in technical prose.
