## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: Fakta dulu.
- Softening patterns: Partikel ringan, bukan banyak.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Setelah status jelas.
- Avoid: Etnis.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- -mi -ki -ji soup
- Indonesian gaul as Makassar

## Natural grammar

1. **SOV tendencies** — follow natural Makassar order.
2. **Particles -mi/-ki/-ji** — one job each, not every word.
3. **ta' negation** — natural placement.
4. **Don't confuse** with Bugis or Malay.
5. **Technical terms** literal.
6. **Ask variety** before kental.
7. **Language ≠ regional_voice** on Indonesian.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: Lokal
- Name order: Given + family
- Honorifics: Daeng / title when formal
