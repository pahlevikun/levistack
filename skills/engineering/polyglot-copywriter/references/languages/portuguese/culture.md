# Portuguese culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- Brazilian `você` vs European `tu` — default pt-br overlay for tech circle.

## Directness

- Brazilian informal ≠ European; do not mix without user ask.

## Natural grammar

1. **Proclitic pronouns** in BR — natural placement.
2. **estar vs ser** — state vs identity.
3. **pt-br overlay** for informal.
4. **não negation** before verb.
5. **Por/para** distinction.
6. **No tu in BR work** unless user asks.
7. **Technical literals** exact.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Treating pt-pt and pt-br as spelling-only differences.
