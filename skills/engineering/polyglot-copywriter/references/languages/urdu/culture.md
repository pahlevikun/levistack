Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: Problem first; respectful close second.
- Softening patterns: براہِ کرم — once in requests.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Dry observation after facts.
- Avoid: Religion, ethnicity, gendered insults.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- English corporate openers
- Fake poetic Arabic filler in tech chat

## Natural grammar

1. **SOV** — `میں فائل پڑھتا ہوں`.
2. **Postpositions** — `فائل میں`, `ٹیبل پر`.
3. **ہے/ہیں agreement** — plural subjects need ہیں.
4. **کر دینا compound** — `چلا دو` for "run it".
5. **آپ verb forms** — honorific plural verb with آپ.
6. **نہیں placement** — before main verb.
7. **Ezāfa** — `API کا token` natural genitive.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: 16 Sep 2026
- Name order: Given + family
- Honorifics: جناب / صاحب / صاحبہ
