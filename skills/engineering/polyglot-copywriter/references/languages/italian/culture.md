# italian culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work email B2B | Lei + title optional | Do not guess closeness from a name |
| Internal chat | tu common among peers | Match user's prior messages |
| External / formal | Lei | Default for incident and customer comms |

## Directness

- First sentence job: lead with problema or beneficio; details follow.
- Softening: `potrebbe`, `per favore` — once per request.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: dry after facts.
- Avoid: regional stereotype mockery, exaggerated hand-gesture clichés in prose.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- `circle back` → `ti aggiorno` / `ti faccio sapere`
- `leverage` calques → verb + object directly

## Natural grammar

1. **Subject–verb agreement** — plural subjects take plural verbs.
2. **Article choice** — il/lo/la/i/gli/le; partitive `del/della` where natural.
3. **Clitic pronouns** — `l'ho visto`, not separated in standard prose.
4. **Passato prossimo vs imperfetto** — completed action vs background state.
5. **Subjunctive after dubitative** — `penso che sia` when doubt is real.
6. **Formal imperative** — `La prego di` in work email; tu imperative in chat.
7. **Punctuation** — no em dash; use comma or new sentence.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: 16/09/2026 or martedì 16 settembre 2026
- Name order: given + family
- Honorifics: Signor/Signora + surname in formal external mail
