# dayak-ngaju culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work chat | Nama + hormat ringan | Do not guess closeness from a name |

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: Fakta dulu.
- Softening patterns: Dari contoh user.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Setelah fakta jelas.
- Avoid: Stereotip.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Bahasa Indonesia dengan sufiks ngaju palsu

## Natural grammar

1. **Do not invent** Ngaju words.
2. **Follow user examples** for word order.
3. **One fact per sentence** when uncertain.
4. **Ask for subdialect** before thick regional forms.
5. **Technical terms** stay literal per repo.
6. **Do not mix** other Borneo languages.
7. **Honesty** beats fake fluency.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: Lokal user
- Name order: Preferensi user
- Honorifics: Tanya jika perlu
