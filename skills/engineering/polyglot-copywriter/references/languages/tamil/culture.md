# tamil culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work email | நீங்கள் + respectful verb forms | Default |
| Peer chat | நீ / casual forms | Only when user already casual |
| Customer external | நீங்கள் | No familiarity guess from name |

## Directness

- First sentence: impact or request; details next.
- Softening: `தயவுசெய்து` once — not every line.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: light after facts.
- Avoid: caste/community stereotypes, mock regional accent in spelling.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- `leverage` → direct Tamil verb
- Passive chains → active voice where natural

## Natural grammar

1. **SOV order** — verb often final; keep one intention per sentence.
2. **Case suffixes** — accurate postpositions (க்கு, இல், ஆல்) for roles.
3. **Verb agreement** — person/number/honorific with subject.
4. **Sandhi** — natural spelling in script; do not break words artificially.
5. **Respectful plural** — நீங்கள் + plural verb in formal work.
6. **Technical terms** — keep API/code tokens; wrap in natural Tamil frame.
7. **Numbers** — use digits for metrics; spell out only when style demands.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: 16-09-2026 or 16 செப்டம்பர் 2026
- Honorifics: அவர்கள் / திரு/திருமதி when formal external
