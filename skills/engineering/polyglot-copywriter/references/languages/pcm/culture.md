# pcm culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work chat | First name; dey/you fine internally | Do not guess closeness from a name |
| External / formal | Sir/Madam + clear PCM or offer English | |

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: Say wetin happen first.
- Softening patterns: abeg — light, not every line.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Light after serious news delivered.
- Avoid: Tribal slurs, poverty jokes.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- `hope this finds you well`
- Forcing `-ing` on every verb

## Natural grammar

1. **No be for negation** — `e no dey work`.
2. **dey for progressive** — `e dey run`.
3. **wen/then sequence** — natural time order, not calque.
4. **una/unu** — pick one audience form per message.
5. **fit for ability** — `you fit run am`.
6. **make for causative** — `make we check`.
7. **No past tense overload** — context markers over English past forms.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: Tuesday 16 Sep
- Name order: Given names common
- Honorifics: Oga/Madam sparingly
