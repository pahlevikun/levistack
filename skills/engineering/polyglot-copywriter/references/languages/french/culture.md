# French culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- `vous` default external; `tu` only when user asked or context is clearly peer.

## Directness

- French ads often softer invite; still one clear CTA.

## Natural grammar

1. **Negation ne…pas** — both parts.
2. **Articles** — le/la/les with natural gender.
3. **Vous verbs** in work external.
4. **On for we** in casual internal OK.
5. **Subjunctive** after il faut que — sparingly.
6. **fr-fr overlay** for lexicon.
7. **Code literals** Latin.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Anglicisms where a normal French word exists in work prose.
