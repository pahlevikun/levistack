# Korean culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- Use job title + 씨 or 팀 context; banmal only with clear peer signal.

## Directness

- Soften refusal; still put technical fact early in incident text.

## Natural grammar

1. **SOV** — verb final.
2. **-요 polite default** in work.
3. **-아/어서 cause** — natural reason.
4. **존댓말/반말** — lock per message.
5. **Topic marker 는/은**.
6. **No English -ing** calque.
7. **Technical literals** exact.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Over-casual English loanwords in formal docs.
