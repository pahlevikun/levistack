# Sunda speech levels

| Level | Config key | When |
|---|---|---|
| Loma | `loma` | Familiar / akrab — default friendly Sundanese |
| Cohag | `cohag` | Deliberately rougher — still no insults to user |

## Ambiguity rule

User says `Sunda kasar` with no extra words → **do not draft**. Ask:

> Do you mean **loma** (akrab) or **cohag** (kasar pisan)?

Both words **loma** and **cohag** must appear in the clarification.

## Boundaries

- `loma` is not an insult register.
- `cohag` ≠ memaki the user. Technical facts stay clear.
- Do not apply to Javanese profiles or English.
