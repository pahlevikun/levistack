## Natural grammar

1. **alus/madia/kasar** locks forms.
2. **nenten/ten negation** by level.
3. **ring locative** — not di default.
4. **No Jawa rek/ik**.
5. **Ceremony register** only when asked.
6. **One level** per message.
7. **Technical literal**.
