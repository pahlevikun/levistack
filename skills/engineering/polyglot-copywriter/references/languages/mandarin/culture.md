# Mandarin culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

- 您 vs 你 by formality; regional overlay picks script and lexicon.

## Directness

- Mainland vs Taiwan vocabulary differs; do not fake either.

## Natural grammar

1. **Topic-comment** — 先说主题再展开.
2. **了/过/着** aspect — 完成用「了」，不要英语过去式直译.
3. **在/于 location** — `在 .env 里`.
4. **把 construction** — `把 token 配好`.
5. **请 vs 麻烦** — 工作里一次就够.
6. **简体/繁体** — overlay 决定，不混写.
7. **技术词** — 保留 repo 字面量.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- Invented 网络用语; English exclamation marketing tone.
