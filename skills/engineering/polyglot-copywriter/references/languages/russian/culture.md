# russian culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work chat | Имя + вы | Do not guess closeness from a name |
| External / formal | Вы + surname or title | |

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: First sentence = fact or ask.
- Softening patterns: Пожалуйста, не могли бы вы — sparingly.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: Understatement after clear diagnosis.
- Avoid: Politics, war jokes, ethnic slurs.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- `hope this finds you well`
- `leverage` → используйте

## Natural grammar

1. **Flexible SVO** — keep natural case marking, don't drop cases.
2. **Aspect** — perfective for completed fixes: `я исправил`.
3. **Negation genitive** — `нет токена`, not `не есть`.
4. **Preposition + case** — `в .env`, `на сервере`.
5. **Impersonal** — `нужно запустить` for instructions.
6. **Diminutives** — not in incident warnings.
7. **Formal passive** — `было обнаружено` for reports.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: 16 сентября 2026 г.
- Name order: Given + family; patronymic in very formal
- Honorifics: Уважаемый / title
