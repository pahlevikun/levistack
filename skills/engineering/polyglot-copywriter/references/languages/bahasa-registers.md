# Bahasa Indonesia Registers

Maps to `register` in [configuration.md](../configuration.md) and the core rules in [core.md](../core.md):

| the author register | Config `register` | When |
|---|---|---|
| Formal (Bahasa Baku) | `baku` | letters, B2B, legal, investor — **on request** |
| Semi-formal | `profesional` | LinkedIn, work email, deck — **on request** |
| Casual (Bahasa Gaul) | `santai` | **default**: chat, everyday writing, simple explanations |

Always apply [core.md](../core.md): simple, specific, active, honest, and never rewrite protected artifacts for style.

Three registers. Pick based on audience and channel before writing.

---

## Register 1: Formal (Bahasa Baku)

**When:** B2B, corporate, banking, insurance, luxury brand, official letters, audience 40+, investor deck

**Pronouns:** Saya / Kami (never "kita" in formal), Anda / Bapak / Ibu

**Verbs:** Full forms — menulis (not nulis), berbicara (not ngomong), memberikan (not kasih)

**Sentences:** Complete, standard SPO structure. No fragments.

**Examples:**
> "Kami menyediakan solusi investasi yang telah terbukti memberikan imbal hasil optimal bagi portofolio Anda."
> "Layanan kami dirancang untuk memenuhi kebutuhan bisnis Anda dengan efisiensi dan keandalan tertinggi."

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Any slang
- ❌ "kita", "lo", "gue"
- ❌ Fragment sentences
- ❌ Emoji (except official brand icons)

---

## Register 2: Semi-formal

**When:** General e-commerce, email marketing, mainstream product landing pages, LinkedIn, newsletter, consumer fintech

**Pronouns:** Kami (brand), Anda / kamu (interchangeable by audience warmth)

**Verbs:** Mixed — "coba" is OK (not "mencoba" exclusively), "pakai" (not always "menggunakan")

**Sentences:** Mostly complete; 1–2 fragments OK for punch.

**Examples:**
> "Cari skincare yang beneran works? Produk kami sudah teruji dermatologis dan dipercaya 50.000+ pengguna."
> "Kirim dalam 2 jam. Gratis ongkir. Tanpa minimum order."

**When to use "kamu" vs "Anda":**
- "Anda" → professional audience, 30+, or brand that wants a premium feel
- "kamu" → younger audience, brand that wants to feel approachable

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Full Gen Z slang ("sabi", "gas", "mantul")
- ❌ Too stiff — like an official letter
- ❌ Long, rambling sentences

---

## Register 3: Casual (Bahasa Gaul)

**When:** Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, WhatsApp blast, Gen Z/Millennial target, FMCG, F&B, lifestyle brand

**Pronouns:** Gue/gw, lo/lu, kita (inclusive)

**Verbs:** Short forms — nulis (not menulis), ngomong (not berbicara), nge-review, nge-order

**Sentences:** Short, fragmentary; one-word lines OK; subject may drop.

**Examples:**
> "Udah capek skincare-an tapi hasilnya gitu-gitu aja? Same. Makanya gue switch ke ini — 2 minggu langsung keliatan bedanya."
> "Lo nggak butuh gym mahal. Lo butuh konsistensi. Dan ini bantu lo sampai di sana."

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Anda", "Bapak/Ibu" — too stiff, breaks the vibe
- ❌ Long formally structured sentences
- ❌ Expired slang (check relevance — slang has a short shelf life)

---

## When to use Gen Z slang

Slang like "sabi", "gas", "cuan", "mantul", "kepo", "healing", "vibes", "literally", "no cap" works for young audiences **only if:**

1. **Natural, not forced** — the brand already talks that way, not trying to sound cool
2. **Brand fit** — banks, insurance, corporate: skip. Lifestyle, FMCG, F&B, gaming: OK.
3. **Slang still alive** — if you are unsure, skip.

**Example that works:**
> "Cuan dari rumah? Gas. Daftar sekarang, dapat cashback 50K."

**Forced example (avoid):**
> "Kami sangat antusias menghadirkan solusi yang super mantul untuk Anda." ← mixed register, sounds odd

## Quick register picker

| Audience | Channel | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Directors, investors, B2B | Email, deck, proposal | Formal |
| Urban professional, 25–40 | LinkedIn, email marketing, web | Semi-formal |
| Gen Z, Millennial | Instagram, TikTok, Twitter | Casual |
| Mixed generations | Everyday writing, chat, simple explain | Casual (default) |
| Official letter / legal | Any | Formal |

When in doubt: casual / `santai` is the default. Upgrade to semi-formal or formal only when the user asks, or when the channel is clearly official (letter, legal, investor).

---

## Cross-register: universal principles still apply

Regardless of register:
- **Specific:** "dipercaya 50.000 pengguna" (not "banyak pengguna")
- **Active:** "Kami kirimkan dalam 2 jam" (not "Pengiriman dilakukan dalam 2 jam")
- **Honest:** Do not claim "No. 1 di Indonesia" without a source — one false claim destroys trust
- **Show, don't tell:** "2 minggu langsung keliatan bedanya" (not "hasilnya luar biasa cepat")
