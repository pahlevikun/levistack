# Bahasa Indonesia culture and transcreation

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Internal work chat (default) | `saya` + `mas`/`kak` | Do not guess closeness from name alone |
| External / HR | `Saya` + `Bapak`/`Ibu` or name | No `mas`/`kak` |
| Jakarta gaul overlay | `gue`/`lo` optional | Only when overlay active and not banned |

## Directness

- Sentence 1 = diagnosis or ask. No `hal ini dikarenakan oleh`, no `yang mana` as English *which*.
- Warmth from `mas`/`kak` and clear verbs, not from long preambles.

## Humor and taboo

- Self-deprecating grind OK in status updates.
- No insulting regional or ethnic stereotypes in city overlays.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- `kindly`, `please revert`, `going forward`, `bandwidth`, calques like `hal ini dikarenakan oleh`

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Dates: `Sen 15 Sep, 10:00 WIB`
- EYD spelling on `baku` only
- `mas`/`kak` are work honorifics, not age guesses for strangers
