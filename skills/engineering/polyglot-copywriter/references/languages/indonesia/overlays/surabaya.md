# surabaya — Surabaya / Arekan overlay

**Type:** `regional` | **Status:** `validated`

## When to load

`language: indonesia` + `regional_voice: surabaya` (Suroboyoan / Arek colour).

## Voice target

Solidarity and Surabaya conversational rhythm without equating bluntness with rudeness. Arek has its own politeness strategies.

## Jobs

| Form | Job | Intensity |
|---|---|---|
| `rek` | Vocative for peers / same-age group | `sedang`+, not every sentence |
| `Cak` | Contextual address | when relationship fits |
| `gak`, `wis`, `durung`, `sek`, `maneh`, `opo`, `nang` | East Javanese rhythm | per intensity |
| `ta` / `to` | Question / confirmation particle | when pragmatic job is clear |
| `koen`/`kon` | Very close address | `kental` only; never guess closeness |
| `sampeyan` | More distance / respect in Arek context | formal-adjacent |
| Blunt first sentence | Direct diagnosis | `tipis`+ (meaning, not insult) |

## Caps

- No insults or taboo words as "authenticity."
- Not Yogya/Solo politeness system.
- Do not mix Boso Walikan (Malang) into Surabaya profile.
- Thick Arekan without examples → cap at `sedang`.
