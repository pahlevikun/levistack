# medan — Medan Indonesian overlay

**Type:** `regional` | **Status:** `validated`

## When to load

`language: indonesia` + `regional_voice: medan`. Multilingual city Indonesian — **not** Bahasa Batak.

## Jobs

| Form | Job | Intensity |
|---|---|---|
| `kali` | Intensifier after the strengthened element | `sedang`+ |
| `lah` | Emphasis, push, or soften a command | `sedang`+ |
| `pula` | Unexpected addition or contrast | `sedang`+, sparingly |
| `aku`/`kau` | When relationship allows | optional |
| Direct opener | Medan plainness | `tipis`+ |

## Caps

- No `bah` on every sentence.
- No `Horas` or Batak costume for all Medan speakers.
- Do not assume `awak`/`kelen` are safe for every relationship.
- Direct ≠ aggressive: no ALL CAPS or extra exclamation marks.
- Do not invent Batak vocabulary as Medan default.
