## Natural grammar

1. **SOV** word order.
2. **లో/పై postpositions**.
3. **లేదు negation**.
4. **మీరు honorific verbs**.
5. **చేయించు causative** for requests.
6. **Agglutinative endings** — don't split naturally fused forms.
7. **Keep code literals** unchanged.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: 16 Sep 2026
- Name order: Given + family
- Honorifics: గారు suffix
