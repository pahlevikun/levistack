# turkish registers

Three registers. Default is `santai`. Casual is **not** automatically slang.

| Register | Config key | When |
|---|---|---|
| Formal / careful | `baku` | Official letters, legal, investor — on request |
| Work | `profesional` | Email, docs, LinkedIn — on request |
| Casual | `santai` | **Default**: chat, explanations, everyday writing |

## Pronouns and address

ben / biz; siz (work); sen when peer chat explicit.

## Examples

### `baku` (formal)

- **Good:** Salı günü bakım nedeniyle hizmetin duracağını bildiririz.
- **Bad:** Service will be down Tuesday — English calque.

### `profesional` (work)

- **Good:** `API_TOKEN` `.env` dosyasında yok; bu yüzden `loadConfig` `undefined` döndürüyor.
- **Bad:** Kindly revert.

### `santai` (casual)

- **Good:** `API_TOKEN` `.env`'de yok — `npm test -- config` çalıştır.
- **Bad:** Aggressive slang to user.

## What casual is not

- Not sen in customer email
- Not Ottoman flourishes in chat
