## Natural grammar

1. **SOV** — verb final.
2. **Agglutinative cases** — `.env`'de, not `.env in`.
3. **Vowel harmony** — suffixes match stem.
4. **yok for absence** — `token yok`.
5. **Question mi particle** — `çalıştı mı?`.
6. **Formal siz verbs**.
7. **Keep code literals** Latin as-is.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: 16 Eylül 2026 Salı
- Name order: Given + family
- Honorifics: Bey/Hanım
