# arz culture and transcreation

Transcreation, not translation. Load when the user asks for cultural fit, ads, or regional colour.

## Address and pronouns

| Situation | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Work chat | First name + light respect | Do not guess closeness from a name |
| External / formal | حضرتك / أستاذ | |

## Directness

- First sentence job in this culture: Point first; warmth in particles, not filler paragraphs.
- Softening patterns: لو سمحت — light.

## Humor and taboo

- Safe: After status is clear.
- Avoid: Politics, religion mockery.

## Do not copy from English corporate

- English email openers
- Fake "Hollywood Arab"

## Natural grammar

1. **مش negation** — not لا only in casual.
2. **هو/هي pronoun copula** — `الخدمة واقفة`.
3. **في for location** — `في .env`.
4. **عايز/محتاج** — need/want distinction in requests.
5. **Present continuous with بـ** — `بيشتغل`.
6. **Question with إيه/ليه** — natural wh-words.
7. **Don't paste MSA case endings** into casual chat.

## Dates, names, honorifics

- Date format: التلات 16 سبتمبر
- Name order: Given + family
- Honorifics: أستاذ / دكتور
