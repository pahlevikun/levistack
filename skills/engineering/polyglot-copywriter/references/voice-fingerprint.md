# Voice Fingerprint (template)

**Optional.** This file captures one person's real writing voice so drafts keep it while the noslop pass thins AI tells. It ships empty. Fill it in from your own writing (reports, messages, reviews, docs), or leave it as is and the skill falls back to [core.md](core.md) and [noslop-prose.md](noslop-prose.md).

Apply **together with** [core.md](core.md) and [noslop-prose.md](noslop-prose.md) on every draft. This file says what to **keep**; noslop says what to **thin**.

**How to fill it:** paste 10 to 30 real samples, then extract only patterns that repeat. Quote short fragments, never whole documents. Remove names, customers, internal service names and anything confidential before saving. Do not include private messages from other people.
