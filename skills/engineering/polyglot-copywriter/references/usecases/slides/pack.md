# slides — use case pack

**Status:** `validated`

## When to load

Deck outline, slide titles + speaker notes in prose.

## Sentence 1 job

Deck title or audience takeaway.

## Section order

1. One slide = one idea (title line)
2. 3–5 bullets max per slide in outline form
3. Speaker note: one sentence why this slide exists

## Default register

`profesional`

## Techniques

Load [marketing-copy.md](../../techniques/marketing-copy.md) § headline formulas for title lines only.

Load [natural-writing.md](../../techniques/natural-writing.md) for speaker notes.

Locale marketing techniques only when deck is ad/pitch — [core.md](../../core.md) § Locale techniques.

## Do not steal from other formats

- Not a full RFC in slide 3.
- Not ad hook rhythm on every title.
