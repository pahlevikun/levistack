# ads — use case pack

**Status:** `validated`

## When to load

Ad copy, short promo, banner text.

## Sentence 1 job

Hook: problem, outcome, or contrast — one line.

## Section order

1. Hook
2. Proof or mechanism (one fact)
3. CTA

## Default register

`profesional` or `santai` per channel; **culture.md** decides hard sell vs soft invite.

## Techniques

Load [marketing-copy.md](../../techniques/marketing-copy.md) § CTA rules and headline formulas (hook line only).

**Inline rules:** one hook, one proof fact, one CTA; no empty superlatives; culture.md sets hard vs soft sell.

## Do not steal from other formats

- Not a landing page (no long sections).
- Not incident comms (no severity theatre).
