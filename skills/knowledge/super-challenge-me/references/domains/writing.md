# Domain: writing

Load after the mode when the subject is prose: spec, RFC narrative, policy, help article, or "grill this document" where the doc is the product.

## Artifacts

Whatever domain docs the repo already uses — glossary, style notes, ADRs, briefs. Do not load a health, SRHR, or creative-writing pack; use this stub + **grill-docs**.

## Lenses

- Who the reader is and what they must be able to do after reading
- Terms that collide with `CONTEXT.md` or house glossary
- Claims that need a source vs decisions the author is making
- What can be cut without losing the decision
- Tone/register only if the repo already has a voice rule

## Do not

- Rewrite the whole doc unasked; grill first, then patch what they accept.
- Duplicate `polyglot-copywriter` unless they ask for a register pass after the grill.
