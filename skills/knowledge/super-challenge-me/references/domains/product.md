# Domain: product

Load after the mode when the subject is an offering, packaging, marketplace, or "should we build this product."

## Artifacts

Pitch, PRD fragment, feature request, landing-page copy used as a spec. New vs existing is a **brainstorm** track, not a second domain.

## Lenses

- Who pays / who uses, and whether those are the same person
- Why now, and what happens if you wait a quarter
- Smallest shippable slice vs the pitch
- Existing product: what must not break, who already depends on the old behavior
- New product: what you will explicitly not build
- Success metric you can observe without a fantasy dashboard

## Do not

- Invent the tech stack (`super-tech-blueprint`).
- Write tickets until they ask (`create-jira-story`).
- Run a 10-persona workshop; four approaches then a rec is enough.
