# Worked example: CFO of AI capacity

Fictional campaign brief for skill practice. Not a real client claim.

## Source

- Topic: budget AI capacity before granting autonomous execution
- Audience: operators and company leaders using agents
- Platform: Pinterest, 1000 × 1500

## Evidence pack

| Claim or asset | Status | Source | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity should be budgeted before autonomous execution | Operating principle | Internal model in brief | Not a universal benchmark |
| The visual has no performance statistic | Verified | Final copy | No invented savings |

## Five-second takeaway

After five seconds, the viewer should understand that autonomous execution must pass through a budget threshold.

## Visual decision

- Primary job: explain
- Relationship: many possible actions narrow into one budget decision
- Layout: convergence (see composition guide in the infographic skill)
- Dominant element: thin paths converging into one gate, then one execution line
- Canvas: warm paper tone from project tokens
- Production: deterministic vector and type

## Copy

- Title: `Autonomy starts at a limit.` (localize per audience)
- Labels: `budget`, `execution`, `evidence`
- Source line: `DAY ONE · AI capacity CFO`
