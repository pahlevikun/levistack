# Review the real artifact

Inspect the render and source together. Report findings by their effect on meaning, then reading, then visual finish. A numerical score does not override a factual or accessibility failure.

## Meaning

- Does the main claim follow from the source, within its stated population and period?
- Can every data mark, quotation and screenshot be traced to a source or a disclosed editorial choice?
- Are raw data, derived values and interpretation distinguished?
- Are units, denominators, scale, rounding and missing values clear?
- Are uncertainty and restrictions close to the claim they qualify?
- Are comparisons framed consistently without invented causation?

An unsupported claim, fabricated UI or invented number blocks delivery as factual output. Correct the claim, acquire the evidence or label a requested mockup synthetic in the asset itself.

## Reading and access

- Can the reader identify the subject and start of the reading path quickly?
- Does the dominant visual explain a relation beyond repeating the headline?
- Are labels, source notes and caveats readable at final display size?
- Are text, meaningful marks and states distinguishable on the actual background and without hue alone?
- Is there a text description or data table for the key information?
- On web, does keyboard access reach every interactive detail and does zoom preserve content?
- Do reduced motion and static output preserve the complete claim?

Use measured evidence for contrast claims. Inspect mobile, desktop or physical print size as applicable. A screenshot from one viewport cannot prove every breakpoint works.

## Composition and craft

Check grid alignment, annotation placement, text fit, visual priority and consistent encoding. Remove generic decoration that competes with the subject. Keep meaningful source artifacts and deliberate brand choices. Do not impose a universal color, typeface or historical style.

For batches, inspect a thumbnail sheet. Check continuity, useful variation and honest shared scales. A repeated layout can be correct for comparable data. A repeated generic layout that hides different relationships needs to change.

## Report and repair

Each finding names the affected mark or text, the problem, why it changes understanding and a concrete fix. Classify unsupported meaning or inaccessible essential evidence first. Distinguish observed failures from checks that were not run.

After a fix, rerun the relevant calculation or export and inspect the changed artifact. Keep a receipt with source paths, versions, dimensions, preview evidence and remaining limits. A final read should verify copy, number, source and caveat together.
