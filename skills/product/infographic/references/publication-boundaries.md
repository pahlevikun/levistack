# Publication boundaries

Use when shipping an infographic to a public channel, open repo, or client handoff, or when adapting this workflow to another brand.

## What this skill shares

A transferable method:

- Build an evidence ledger before designing visuals.
- Use grid and layout as an argument, not decoration.
- Keep source type, transform, caveat, and rights visible on or beside the mark.
- Treat color, type, spacing, and motion as rules tied to roles or tokens.
- Review claim integrity before polish.

The method applies to archives, policy explainers, research posters, data journalism, museum interpretation, and editorial dashboards.

## What it does not grant

This skill does not grant reuse of:

- A project-specific visual identity or logo system.
- Brand names, authorship marks, or trademarked trade dress from a client.
- Curated datasets, classifications, annotations, or copy from a supplied example.
- Third-party source material cited in the user's project.
- Restricted corpus, clinical, legal, or publisher material without rights clearance.

Copy the reasoning pattern. Do not clone a finished identity from an example or reference project.

## Public or client release checklist

Before sharing outside the team:

- Remove private notes, internal paths, and draft placeholders.
- Confirm examples use fictional or cleared data only.
- List rights for photos, screenshots, fonts, and data.
- Ship alt text, source ledger, and caveats with the asset.
- Run review and publishing-gate when copy ships with the image (see SKILL.md router).
- State which marks are measured versus not verified.
